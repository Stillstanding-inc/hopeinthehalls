Hope in the Halls

Bringing hope, prayer, and presence into the halls where people are hurting.

"I was sick and you visited me." — Matthew 25:36

What this is

Hope in the Halls is the hospital-visitation heart of Still Standing. It exists for the people in the hard rooms and the long corridors — the patient in the bed, the family in the waiting room, the one who just got the news and doesn't know what to do with it. We show up. We pray with anybody who wants prayer. We sit with people so nobody in those halls has to feel alone or forgotten.

It was born out of hard places personally known — years of being the one lying there wondering if anybody was coming. Somebody came. That's why we go.

What we bring

  • Prayer — for any patient or family who wants it, in the hall, at the bedside, or over the phone
  • Presence — a listening ear and unhurried company in a place that can feel very lonely
  • Encouragement — the word that you're still standing, and you're not standing alone
  • Connection — families pointed to real help with food, bills, and getting back on their feet through the Still Standing Help Line

If someone you love is in the hospital and you'd like a visit or prayer, reach out. If you serve in a hospital — staff, chaplaincy, or administration — and you'd welcome a visit ministry walking your halls, we'd be glad to talk.
